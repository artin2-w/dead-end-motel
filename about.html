About Dead End Motel

Dead End Motel is a browser-based management and survival strategy game.

  • Players manage motel operations, guest decisions, room pressure, power, camera feeds, and night reports.
  • The project is made for entertainment and learning purposes.
  • The game runs directly in the browser and does not require downloads.
