                    About the Game

                    Dead End Motel is a browser-based motel management and survival strategy game where players manage guests, rooms, power, and incidents during intense night shifts.

                    How to Play

                    Manage guests: Each arrival is a decision. Read the desk information, verify what you can, and choose whether to check in, flag, or reject.

                    Use cameras: Scan the feeds to spot anomalies early. Cameras help you catch pressure before it spreads into rooms and shared spaces.

                    Handle incidents: Incidents and room calls build momentum. Respond quickly, prioritize the highest-risk problems, and avoid letting multiple zones go critical at once.

                    Manage power and pressure: Power is your survival budget. Heavy actions can drain the grid, and rising pressure makes mistakes cost more.

                    Survive the night: Keep the motel stable until dawn by spending attention where it matters most.

                    Tips

                    • Stay calm under pressure: When alerts spike, slow down and make one clean decision at a time.
                    • Monitor guest behavior: Contradictions and risk signals matter more than flavor text.
                    • Balance power usage: Avoid stacking scans and heavy actions when the reserve is low.
                    • Avoid mistakes during high tension: The late night punishes rushed clicks—prioritize containment over “perfect” outcomes.
